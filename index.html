Engineered with Care

Every feature is crafted strictly to serve your worship and obligations.

🕌

Accurate Astronomical Calculations

Computes solar angles with offline mathematical calculation and intelligent caching. Supports Muslim World League, Makkah, ISNA, Egypt, Karachi, Gulf, and Moon Sighting conventions.

🧭

3D Tilt-Compensated Qibla

Orientation sensor fusion with magnetic declination correction. Accurately pinpoints the Holy Kaaba in Makkah whether your phone is held upright or flat on a table.

💰

4-Step Zakat Walkthrough

Wizard-guided calculation for gold (85g) or silver (595g) Nisab. Dynamic multi-carat gold purity breakdown (24k, 22k, 21k, 18k, 14k), live currency valuation, and Hawl calendar reminder.

📅

Hijri Calendar & Fasting

Side-by-side Gregorian and Hijri monthly calendar with major Islamic events, plus voluntary White Days (Ayyam al-Beed) indicators and eve-of-fast reminders.

🔔

Downloadable Adhans & Exact Alarms

Curated audio adhans from Makkah, Madinah, Al-Aqsa, and Cairo with SHA-256 integrity verification. Exact Android alarms that reliably survive phone reboots and timezone shifts.

🌙

Emerald-Night Theme

Custom-built dark palette with a subtle container ladder, emerald accents, and warm brass countdowns. Engineered for low-light mosques and optimal battery efficiency.
