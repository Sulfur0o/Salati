What it does

Prayer times

Astronomical times for a city you pick with GPS or search. Methods include Muslim World League, Umm al-Qura, ISNA, Egypt, Karachi, and others. Asr by madhab. Midnight and last third of the night for Qiyam.

Qibla

Direction to the Kaaba from the saved location. Works held flat or upright, with magnetic declination.

Hijri calendar

Gregorian and Hijri side by side. Islamic New Year, Ashura, Ramadan, the two Eids, Day of Arafah, and optional White Days reminders.

Zakat

Cash, gold by karat, and silver. Nisab from gold (85 g) or silver (595 g). Metal prices come from a public currency feed and are approximate.

Adhan and alarms

Optional downloaded recitations, attributed in Settings. Exact alarms when Android allows them; otherwise the system may delay the notice. Restored after reboot. Some phones still need battery restrictions lifted.

Offline months

Once a month is on the device, that month still works without a network. If a later month is missing, times can be computed on the phone.

Privacy. There is no Salati account and no Salati server. Configured coordinates are sent to the Aladhan prayer-times API only when a month is downloaded. Settings (including location and any Zakat figures) can be included in Android Backup. Full policy.
