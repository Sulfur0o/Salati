1. Core Privacy Principles

Salati is designed from the ground up to respect your spiritual life and your personal data. We operate under strict principles:

  • No User Accounts: Salati does not require, support, or use accounts, registrations, passwords, or emails.
  • No Advertisements: Zero ads, marketing trackers, or third-party advertising SDKs.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not include any tracking tools, behavioral analytics, telemetry SDKs, or crash-reporting trackers.
  • No Data Monetization: We never sell, rent, broker, or use personal information for commercial exploitation.
  • Sandboxed Local Storage: Preferences, prayer adjustments, configured coordinates, and cached calculations remain strictly within Android's private app sandbox.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

Salati requests only the minimal set of permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core functionality:

A. Location Permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Purpose: To determine your geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) solely to compute local astronomical prayer times and the 3D Qibla direction towards the Holy Kaaba in Makkah. When location is detected via GPS, the Android platform Geocoder API is invoked locally to resolve human-readable city names.

User Control: Location permission is strictly optional. You are never prompted for location access until you explicitly tap "Detect my location". You can skip GPS entirely during onboarding, keep the default coordinates, or search and enter your city name manually without granting location permissions.

Background Access: Salati never reads or tracks location in the background. Coordinates are read only upon explicit user interaction in the foreground.

B. Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)

Purpose: On Android 13 (API 33) and higher, this permission allows Salati to deliver on-time prayer alerts, approaching prayer warnings, and voluntary White Days (Ayyam al-Beed) fasting reminders.

User Control: You maintain complete granular control. In app Settings, you can mute all notifications at once, customize pre-prayer warning offsets (0–30 minutes), and independently configure sound and vibration per prayer.

C. Exact Alarms Permission (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)

Purpose: Islamic prayers must be observed at exact times. Salati uses Android's Exact Alarm API solely to schedule precise local alarm intents for prayer notifications.

Resilience: If access is denied, Salati automatically falls back to inexact alarms and continues operating safely. Alarms are restored locally upon device reboot without requiring network connectivity.

D. Notification Policy Access (ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY)

Purpose: Used only if you optionally enable "Prayer Silent Mode". Salati checks that policy access is granted, silences the ringer for a bounded prayer window after the Adhan, and promptly restores your previous ringer mode. It never leaves the device in a permanent silent or Do Not Disturb state.

3. External Network Communications

To compute prayer times, download adhan audio, and provide precious metal prices for Zakat, Salati communicates with public network endpoints:

  1. Prayer Times API (Aladhan, api.aladhan.com): Salati downloads monthly prayer schedules using the configured latitude, longitude, year, month, calculation method, school, and high-latitude rule. No user profile, device identifier, advertising ID, or email address is sent.
  2. Curated Adhan Audio (salati.sulfuro.xyz): When you choose to download an authentic Adhan, audio files are retrieved over HTTPS and verified locally against cryptographic SHA-256 hashes before playback. No tracking headers are sent.
  3. Currency Exchange Feed (cdn.jsdelivr.net / latest.currency-api.pages.dev): When refreshing metal prices in the Zakat calculator, Salati requests public exchange-rate files. Coordinates are not included in this request.

All network connections use HTTPS. Like any standard web request, server operators receive standard HTTP connection metadata (such as the device's public IP address). Salati does not operate a developer backend and does not intercept or collect this data.

4. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

All data retained by Salati (such as saved city coordinates, calculation parameters, alarm preferences, and cached timetables) lives exclusively in your device's private app sandbox.

  • Cloud Backup Disabled: Android automated cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of app data are intentionally disabled (allowBackup="false") to safeguard your preferences.
  • Instant Deletion: You can completely erase all app data at any time by clearing storage in Android Settings, or simply by uninstalling the application.
